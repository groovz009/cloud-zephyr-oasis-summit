# The Marriage Reset — Interior Image Directions (Photoreal revision)

Grace & Harmony Press · ivory `#F9F4EE` + red `#FD1915`

Revision: week openers and bonus title plates are now **photoreal human stills**, not line-art ornaments. The clasped-hands heart mark is retired from these eight plates (it may still live on the cover and as a tiny typeset divider on daily pages). No body copy is baked into the photographs. Headings land in Step 5 in the reserved ivory field.

## Shared visual system

- Ground: warm ivory / cream `#F9F4EE`. Airy daylight. Never dark or moody.
- Accent: deep brand red `#FD1915` only — mug rim, cuff, door edge, folded throw. One accent per frame.
- Wardrobe / set: ivory, cream, white, soft charcoal. Skin and hair as they are.
- People: real adult couples. Mixed ages and ethnicities across the eight plates. Modest clothing. No gaze at camera.
- Type later: all titles typeset in Step 5 over the empty cream field.
- Ban: line-art hearts on these plates; wedding gowns; tuxedos; altars; crisis crying shots; readable handwriting; scripture on props.

Composition: tall portrait. Human moment in the upper half. Lower 40–50% empty ivory.
